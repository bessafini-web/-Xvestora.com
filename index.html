Legal & Administrative Access

The land you want may be legally out of reach.
We know the route that makes it reachable.

In Morocco, what a foreign investor may acquire depends less on the asset than on the structure it is acquired through. Three routes, three regimes.

In your own name

Urban property and building plots inside the urban perimeter, registered with the ANCFCC. Direct acquisition, title held in your name.

Through a Moroccan company

Under Law 62-19, a Moroccan-law société anonyme or société en commandite par actions may acquire agricultural land without limitation, including where the capital is entirely foreign-held. The legal form is decisive: an SARL does not qualify.

Reclassification

Agricultural land becomes directly acquirable once an attestation de vocation non agricole has been obtained. An administrative route, not a formality.

Every file passes through lawyers, notaries, chartered accountants and adoul before it reaches you — title verification with the ANCFCC, corporate structuring, authentication of the deed, tax treatment. We sit on the buyer's side of the table, as though the acquisition were our own. A file that would expose our investor is a file we decline.

Investment Charter

What the State contributes.

Morocco does not only allow foreign investment. Under the Investment Charter it co-finances it. Most investors never claim what they are entitled to, because the eligibility is read after the acquisition rather than before it.

01

Main scheme

Open to projects of at least 50 million MAD creating 50 stable jobs, or to projects creating more than 150 stable jobs with no minimum investment. Premiums are cumulative and capped at 30% of the eligible investment: 5–10% on employment and capital expenditure, 10% or 15% territorial depending on the province, 5% sectoral, and 3% each for gender approach, future professions, sustainable development and local integration.

02

Small and mid-sized enterprises

Turnover between 1 and 200 million MAD, investment between 1 and 50 million, at least 1.5 stable jobs per million invested and at least 10% equity financing. Employment premium of 5%, 7% or 10% according to jobs created, 10% or 15% territorial, 10% sectoral. Same 30% cap.

03

Strategic projects

A dedicated regime applies from 2 billion MAD.

04

Tax and customs

Five-year corporate tax exemption for exporters and for companies established in industrial acceleration zones, followed by a reduced rate. VAT exemption on imported capital equipment during the first twenty-four months. Customs relief on production equipment.

05

Getting your money out

The exchange regime guarantees the free transfer of net profits, of the proceeds of disposal or liquidation, and of interest on investment-related loans.

Eligibility is assessed case by case, and depends on how the acquisition is structured. That assessment belongs before the purchase, not after it.

FAQ

Everything you need to know
before investing.

What is the minimum investment ticket to work with Xvestora?

There is no fixed ticket size. We work on deals from 500K USD upwards. What matters is the seriousness of the approach, not just the amount. If your budget is still to be defined, submit a brief anyway — we will assess feasibility together.

Are the opportunities truly off-market?

Yes, 100%. We do not work with listing portals or aggregators. Every asset presented to our members is sourced through our private network: landowners, notaries, developers and trusted intermediaries. These plots are not, and have not been, publicly listed.

How long does the process take, from brief to signing?

It depends on deal complexity and your responsiveness. Based on our recent transactions: between 4 and 8 months from initial brief to notarial signing. Key steps: profile validation, targeted sourcing, due diligence, negotiation, notarial deed.

Does Xvestora work with non-resident investors in Morocco?

Yes. The majority of our clients are based outside Morocco — Gulf, Europe, Sub-Saharan Africa. We are well-versed in non-resident regulatory requirements (capital repatriation, investment code, notarial obligations) and coordinate with local legal counsel as needed.

How is Xvestora compensated?

Our fees vary according to the service and the complexity of the operation. They are agreed between the parties, within the framework of Moroccan law and the rules of international commerce, and the terms are established and accepted before any commitment.
