FAQ

Everything you need to know
before investing.

What is the minimum investment ticket to work with Xvestora?

There is no fixed ticket size. We work on deals from 500K USD upwards. What matters is the seriousness of the approach, not just the amount. If your budget is still to be defined, submit a brief anyway — we will assess feasibility together.

Are the opportunities truly off-market?

Yes, 100%. We do not work with listing portals or aggregators. Every asset presented to our members is sourced through our private network: landowners, notaries, developers and trusted intermediaries. These plots are not, and have not been, publicly listed.

How long does the process take, from brief to signing?

It depends on deal complexity and your responsiveness. Based on our recent transactions: between 4 and 8 months from initial brief to notarial signing. Key steps: profile validation, targeted sourcing, due diligence, negotiation, notarial deed.

Does Xvestora work with non-resident investors in Morocco?

Yes. The majority of our clients are based outside Morocco — Gulf, Europe, Sub-Saharan Africa. We are well-versed in non-resident regulatory requirements (capital repatriation, investment code, notarial obligations) and coordinate with local legal counsel as needed.

How is Xvestora compensated?

Our fees are discussed directly with each client based on the nature and complexity of the operation. We work with full transparency: compensation terms are clarified before any commitment. No hidden fees.
