Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fee to submit a brief?
Our fees vary according to the service and the complexity of the operation. They are agreed between the parties, within the framework of Moroccan law and the rules of international commerce, and the terms are established and accepted before any commitment.
What happens if my profile is not selected?
You will receive an honest answer within 48h. If your profile does not match our current pipeline — in terms of ticket size, sector or geography — we will tell you clearly. You may submit a new brief at any time if your criteria change.
I am a non-resident in Morocco — can I still invest?
Yes. The majority of our clients are based outside Morocco (Gulf, Europe, Africa). We are well-versed in the regulatory constraints for non-resident investors — capital repatriation, the investment code, notarial obligations — and coordinate with local legal counsel.
