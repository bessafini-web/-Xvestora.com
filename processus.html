How It Works

The Process
in 5 Steps.

From the initial brief to the notarised signing — a structured, confidential framework for qualified investors deploying capital into Moroccan land.

01

Entering the Network

Submitting Your Investment Brief

You complete a confidential form in 3 minutes: investor profile, indicative ticket, sectors of interest, target geography and investment horizon. This brief becomes our roadmap. There is no commitment at this stage — only the start of a private conversation.

Online form 100% confidential No commitment
02

Qualification

Validation & Onboarding within 48h

Every application is reviewed individually by our team. We check the consistency of the profile, the seriousness of the approach, and the fit with our network of active sellers. If your profile is accepted, we schedule an initial confidential conversation to refine your criteria and walk you through how the network operates.

Response within 48h Qualification call NDA if required
03

Active Search

Targeted Sourcing Through Our Private Network

Your criteria become our active sourcing mandate. We activate our network of landowners, notaries, developers and trusted intermediaries across Morocco. Unlike a search on public portals, we access assets that are not publicly circulated — land whose owners seek a discreet, fast disposal on balanced terms.

Notarial network Private landowners Unlisted assets All regions of Morocco
04

Verification

Due Diligence & Asset Analysis

Before any opportunity is presented to you, it undergoes a structured review: land title verification, legal status of the plot (melk, registered, in process), planning constraints, zoning, easements, and analysis of local context. You receive a factual file — not a sales pitch. Clarity is our standard.

Title verification Land status Zoning & planning Local market analysis
05

Closing

Negotiation, Deed & Notarised Signing

If the opportunity matches your criteria, we coordinate the negotiation phase and the structuring of the transaction — price, conditions precedent, timeline. We facilitate the introduction to local legal counsel and coordinate through to the signing of the notarised deed. You do not manage the logistics: we do it for you.

Assisted negotiation Legal coordination Notarised deed Full support
