Our 4 Service Lines

What we do
for you.

Four service lines. Five sectors.

01

Off-Market Land Opportunities

We identify and present land assets that are not publicly listed — sourced through our private network of landowners, notaries and trusted intermediaries. Every opportunity is exclusive: you are often the only qualified investor to see it.

Included in this service

  • Active sourcing mandate based on your brief
  • Identification of unlisted assets through the notarial network
  • Confidential presentation before wider circulation
  • Factual file: title, status, zoning, local context
Submit a Brief
02

Strategic Investment Advisory

Market analysis, investment structuring and transaction support across real estate, industrial, agricultural, tourism and energy projects in Morocco — including the legal and administrative structuring the acquisition requires: corporate form, title verification, notarial process and eligibility for State incentives, handled with our lawyers, notaries, chartered accountants and adoul.

Included in this service

  • Market analysis and regional benchmarking
  • Development potential assessment
  • Legal and tax structuring (coordination)
  • Support on negotiation and terms
Discuss a Project
03

Turnkey Project Development

From land acquisition to operational delivery: we coordinate design, permitting, construction and commissioning. For investors who want to delegate the entire process and receive an asset ready to generate income.

Included in this service

  • Land acquisition through our off-market network
  • Coordination of architects, engineering firms, permits
  • Construction oversight and site monitoring
  • Commissioning and post-delivery support
Discuss a Turnkey Project
04

Private Investor Network

For larger operations, we facilitate introductions between qualified co-investors — family offices, investment funds, HNWIs with aligned criteria. Every introduction is discreet, targeted and based on genuine compatibility between profiles.

Included in this service

  • Access to the Xvestora investor network (Gulf, Europe, Africa)
  • Discreet, targeted introductions based on profile
  • Structuring of co-investment arrangements
  • Facilitation of discussions and NDA coordination
